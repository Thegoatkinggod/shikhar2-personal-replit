Investment Philosophy Page

Title: Investment Philosophy

Introduction:

My approach to investing is grounded in fundamental, bottom-up analysis with a long-term ownership mindset. Rather than viewing stocks as tickers on a screen to be traded for short-term gains, I view them as fractional ownership stakes in real businesses.

Core Principles
Focus on Quality Moats

I seek businesses protected by strong structural barriers to entry, such as high switching costs, network effects, brand equity, or cost advantages.

Financial Resilience

I prioritize companies with healthy balance sheets, robust free cash flow generation, and high returns on invested capital.

Long-Term Orientation

Markets are often driven by short-term noise, quarterly earnings pressure, and macro sentiment. I aim to capitalize on opportunities where a company's long-term compounding potential is undervalued.

Margin of Safety

Valuation matters. A disciplined investment process requires buying at a price that offers asymmetric upside while protecting against permanent capital loss.
